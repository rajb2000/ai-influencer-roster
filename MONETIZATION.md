# MONETIZATION.md — the 5-stream playbook

All figures are **labeled estimates, not promises**.

**Honest baseline (the whole roster, combined):**
- **Months 0–3: $0–20/week.** Most accounts earn nothing at all in this window.
- **Months 3–6: $50–150/week** — and only if posting stays daily and consistent.

**CORRECTION 2026-10-17:** the per-stream $/wk figures in this file were 3–5x too optimistic (verified via the Harmony review — recorded in LEARN.md). They've been cut down below. The new rule: the per-stream numbers must fit inside the combined baseline above — they can't add up to more than the honest total.

## Stream 1 — Brand deals
- **Unlocks at:** 5–10K followers for inbound; micro-deals from ~1K in tight niches. UGC-style deals (brands pay for the content, not the reach) can pay $50–500/post even small.
- **Leads:** Vera (senior wellness brands — the most under-served sponsor lane in the roster) · Mila (fintech/broker apps pay the highest CPMs) · Esther (PG relationship/coaching brands) · Libby (American-made brands).
- **Free tooling:** organic DM/email outreach, media kit built in Canva free.
- **Timeline (est):** first deals typically months 4–9 — so usually **$0 in the 0–6 month window**. If one lands early: maybe $25–75/wk in a lucky month.
- **Blunt:** episodic income, negotiation-heavy; engagement rate matters more than follower count. Don't count on this early.

## Stream 2 — Physical products (print-on-demand, zero upfront)
- **Unlocks at:** any follower count — but needs traffic to convert, so realistically 2K+.
- **Leads:** Libby (flag/slogan merch — the strongest merch lane in the roster) · Mila (paper-trade journal/planner — genuinely useful, fits the underdog grind) · Esther (statement wholesome merch later).
- **Free tooling:** Printful/Printify free plans — no monthly fee, you pay production cost only when an order lands; sell via free link-in-bio page.
- **Timeline (est):** months 3–6. **$/wk: $0–10** early (margins are $3–8/item — needs volume).
- **Blunt:** thin margins; this is a loyalty play, not a money printer. Never pre-buy inventory.

## Stream 3 — Ad revenue
- **Blunt first:** for an Instagram-only roster this stream is **~$0**. Instagram Reels bonuses are invite-only and shrinking; Meta killed broad Reels Play payouts. YouTube long-form pays real ad revenue, but that's a different content game than this roster plays.
- **Unlocks at:** n/a for IG. Revisit only if a winning persona expands to YouTube (1K subs + 4K watch hours for Partner Program).
- **$/wk: $0** for the foreseeable future. This section exists so nobody fantasizes about it.

## Stream 4 — Digital products
- **Unlocks at:** any count; needs trust, so realistically months 3+.
- **Leads:** Mila (options-basics ebook, paper-trade journal template) · Vera (senior strength starter guide) · Esther (conversation-starters pack, dating profile teardown guide) · Libby (family budgeting worksheets).
- **Free tooling:** Gumroad free tier ($0 upfront, they take a cut per sale); design in Canva free.
- **Timeline (est):** launch months 3–6, meaningful months 6–12. **$/wk: $0–25** early (one $19–29 guide that actually converts can beat that — but plan for zero until it does).
- **Blunt:** historically this is where the real money is for most AI personas — not brand deals. One guide that converts beats a year of micro-sponsored posts. But "real money" still lives inside the $50–150/wk combined ceiling for months 3–6.

## Stream 5 — Affiliate income
- **Unlocks at:** immediately — even 500 followers.
- **Leads:** Mila (broker/finance affiliates — the highest RPMs in the game) · Vera (senior wellness gear — trusted voice, under-served buyers) · Esther (dating books, coaching programs — PG only).
- **Free tooling:** Amazon Associates (1–10%), direct program signups — all free to join. Disclose every link (FTC rules).
- **Timeline (est):** first clicks months 2–4. **$/wk: $0–25** early; scales with audience (industry math: 10K followers → ~$150–450/mo, an estimate).
- **Blunt:** easiest to start, slowest to scale; reads as spam without genuine trust, so it rides behind the content, never ahead of it.

## What never happens
Paid followers, paid ads, paid tools. Free-only is a hard rule — if a step needs money, it gets a free path or it gets dropped.
