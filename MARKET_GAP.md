# MARKET_GAP.md — competition density in health / wealth / relationships + USP whitespace

Researched 2026-10-17. Figures are reported/industry-estimated. Frame under exploration: 3 niches (health, wealth, relationships), each persona differentiated by one USP. Raj makes the final call.

## Rate anchors
- Sponsored posts: nano (1–10K) $25–150/post · micro (10–50K) $100–600 · mid (50–200K) $500–2K · macro (200K+) $2–9K+. Video pays 50–100% over static.
- Niche multipliers: finance 1.4–1.5x baseline; luxury fashion/beauty +20–60%.
- Affiliate math (est): 10K followers → $150–450/mo; 100K → $1,200–3,600/mo.
- Brands consider AI partnerships from 5–10K followers; engagement rate beats follower count.
- First brand deal typically 90–120 days post-launch. Baseline $0 for months.

## The 5-persona map vs the gaps
1. Mila Penn — wealth × underdog → open niche × open USP. Strongest combo on paper: highest CPMs + most shareable story shape.
2. Rhea Blaze — health × supermodel → medium-thin niche × partially contested USP, but the fitness+editorial overlap is unclaimed.
3. Health × old age — medium-thin niche × open USP (for a trusted, openly-AI player). Senior wellness is a top-3 affiliate category.
4. Relationships × high maintenance — open × open. Bluest water in the roster.
5. Wealth × patriotic — open × open. American-made brand lane + high finance CPMs.

## Honest risks
- USP differentiation only matters if the content proves it weekly — a USP on paper with generic posts is just a costume.
- Old age: the lane has spam history (fake fit grandmas) — the openly-AI + genuinely useful positioning is the defense.
- Patriotic: narrow audience ceiling vs broad wealth content; strong engagement within it.
- 5 personas = 5 daily content engines. That's the real constraint — the crown rules (top 2 at day 30) exist to kill the workload, not just the losers.
