## Crown rules (Raj's 30-day call)
- **Day 30:** rank all four by **follower growth rate** (followers gained per week, not total). Keep the **top 2**. **Kill the rest** — pause their accounts, archive their folders, move all effort into the winners.
- **Tie-break:** higher engagement rate wins.
- **Early kill (any time):** a persona that posts consistently for 14 days and gains <100 followers is a dead lane — kill early, don't wait for day 30.
- **After the crown:** the winners get the full effort — daily posts, reels with the TTS voice profiles, affiliate link setup, brand outreach at 5–10K followers.
