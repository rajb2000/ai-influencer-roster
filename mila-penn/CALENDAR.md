## Pillars (from PERSONA.md)

Why this survives past week 2:
- **Pulses** ride the morning orchestrator's premarket read — data already exists, she just narrates it. Zero new research.
- **Ledgers** ride the paper P&L track — one honest number a day, especially losses.
- **Lessons** batch-write 4 at a time on a Sunday; queue them. Beginner concepts never run out.
- **Tool Friday** is one tool per week, from a fixed list below. Screenshot-friendly, no hype.
