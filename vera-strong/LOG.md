**How to log:**
- **Date** — the day it posted.
- **Post** — short title, matching the calendar.
- **Format** — single image, carousel, reel, text, story.
- **Hook** — the opening line or viral-map hook used.
- **Followers** — total follower count after this post.
- **Engagement rate** — (likes + comments) ÷ followers × 100.
- **Notes** — what worked, what flopped. Be honest: flops go in the log too.

**Weekly extraction:** every Sunday, read the week's rows into the persona's LEARN.md — one line per pattern found (e.g., "carousel > single image for saves, 3/3 weeks"). What gets measured gets monetized.
